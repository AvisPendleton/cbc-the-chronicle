The newspaper

Five ways to contribute

Send us original work, news tips, personal perspectives, creative pieces, or links to worthwhile reading.

01 / CAMPUS

Campus News

News and developments affecting our school and campus community.

  • School announcements and policy changes
  • Campus events, clubs and student activities
  • Facilities, services and student resources
  • Student achievements and community initiatives
02 / COMMUNITY

Student Voices

Personal experiences and perspectives from students and the wider school community.

  • Personal stories and student experiences
  • Interviews with students and staff
  • Student life, challenges and opportunities
  • Cultural backgrounds and school life
03 / CULTURE

Arts & Cultures

Creative work, cultural events, and thoughtful engagement with art and culture.

  • Book, film, music and performance reviews
  • Exhibitions and cultural events
  • Creative writing, photography and visual art
  • Cultural traditions and intercultural experiences
04 / KNOWLEDGE

Academics & Ideas

A place for curiosity, research, learning and interesting discoveries.

  • Student research and academic projects
  • Science, history, sociology and philosophy
  • Essays and educational resources
  • External articles, reports and research links
05 / COMMENTARY

Opinions & Editorials

Arguments, commentary and considered responses to issues that matter.

  • Opinion pieces and critical commentary
  • Perspectives on current issues
  • Education and school-policy debates
  • Responses to previously published work
Sharing something you found online? Use Academics & Ideas, include the original link, and briefly explain why you think it is worth sharing. Please credit the original author and publisher.
